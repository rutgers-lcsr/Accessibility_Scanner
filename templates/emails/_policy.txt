Why this is required. Rutgers University Policy 70.1.5 requires university websites to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/). The U.S. Department of Justice's ADA Title II web accessibility rule sets the same standard for public universities and has been in effect for Rutgers since April 24, 2026. The issues in this report are automated checks against that standard. Questions about the requirement: accessibility@rutgers.edu.
